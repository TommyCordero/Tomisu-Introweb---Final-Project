History of Sweetness

Tomisu was born during the pandemic—a time when people longed for comfort, connection, and a little sweetness to brighten their days. What started as a passion project soon became a way to share joy through one of the world’s most beloved desserts: tiramisu.

At Tomisu, we believe every slice should be more than just dessert—it should be a moment of happiness. Inspired by the timeless Italian classic and enriched with Filipino creativity, our mission is to bring you tiramisu flavors that comfort, excite, and connect people in every layer. Whether it’s for yourself, family, or as a gift, Tomisu makes every moment sweeter. With just a few clicks, you can explore our menu and order now!
